Privacy Policy

A clear note about your privacy in this frontend demonstration.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Dear Visitor,

Thank you for visiting Bump & Bloom. The information below explains how this front-end demonstration operates. This sample document should be reviewed and updated with verified company details before any real retail launch.

1. What this website is

Bump & Bloom is a demonstration storefront featuring illustrative products, fictional leadership profiles and sample customer reviews. This version does not operate a verified online retail service.

2. Information you enter

Contact forms collect the name, email, phone number, city, enquiry topic and message you choose to provide. Newsletter forms collect the email address you enter. These details are saved locally in your browser for demonstration purposes; they are not transmitted to Bump & Bloom by this frontend alone.

3. Local browser storage

This demo uses browser localStorage to remember theme preference, reading direction, shopping cart, saved enquiries and newsletter sign-ups. You can clear those records using your browser site-data settings. The demo has no server-based checkout or order-processing backend.

4. Third-party content

Google Fonts and a Google Maps embedded preview may connect to their respective providers when the page is loaded, subject to network access and their own privacy practices. The map shows a sample city centre, not a verified store location.

5. Retention and choices

Browser-saved demo data remains on the device until you clear the site data or the browser removes it. Do not include sensitive medical or payment information in sample enquiry forms. The site does not intentionally request payment card details.

6. Updates and contact

This sample policy may be revised as real services are added. A verified business contact address, data controller details and lawful processing disclosures must be supplied before production use.

Warm regards,
The Bump & Bloom Demo Team

For questions about the demo, please use the contact form. A real business support contact must be added before launch.
